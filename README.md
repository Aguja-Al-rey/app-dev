# My Favorite Movie Series: Harry Potter

## About
**Harry Potter** is a fantasy movie series based on the books by *J.K. Rowling*. It shows a young wizard named Harry and his friends Hermione and Ron at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

## Films
1. Sorcerer's Stone (2001)
2. Chamber of Secrets (2002)
3. Prisoner of Azkaban (2004)
4. Goblet of Fire (2005)
5. Order of the Phoenix (2007)
6. Half-Blood Prince (2009)
7. Deathly Hallows Part 1 (2010)
8. Deathly Hallows Part 2 (2011)

## Favorite Characters
- Severus Snape
- Sirius Black
- Fred and George Weasley
- Harry Potter
